# Guide: General Pharmacy cashier (T1)

This guide is for the cashier at the General Pharmacy counter, till **T1**. It covers a full day: signing in, opening the shift, selling, taking payment, printing receipts, working when the internet is down, and closing the shift.

Buttons and messages are written here **in bold**, exactly as they appear on the screen.

## 1. Sign in

1. Open Chrome on the till PC. The clinic's web address opens: https://\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_
2. On **Log in to your account**, type your **Email address** and **Password**.
3. Press **Log in**. You go straight to the till screen.

Each person has their own account. Never use someone else's account, and never tell anyone your password. Every sale records who rang it up.

The till does not sign you out by itself. **Sign out** when you leave the counter (but not while sales are waiting to sync, section 14), so nobody sells under your name.

## 2. Choose the till (first time only)

The first time you sign in on a PC, the screen says **Choose a till**.

1. Press **T1** (General Pharmacy).
2. The browser remembers it. You will not be asked again on this PC.

You can only choose a till at a counter you are allowed to work at. If no till is shown, ask the Admin.

## 3. Open your shift

At the start of your shift the till shows **Open a shift on T1**.

1. Count the cash in the drawer.
2. Type it in **Opening cash (LKR)**, for example `5000`.
3. Press **Open shift**.

The top of the screen now shows **Shift open**.

Always open a shift: the till does not sell without one. It asks for the opening cash before the first sale.

## 5. Find an item

The search box says **Scan barcode or search generic, brand, strength, SKU (F2)**.

**Scan the barcode** on the box or pack. The item goes straight onto the bill.

- Scanning the barcode of a **box** adds one box.
- Scanning the barcode of a **single unit** adds one tablet, vial and so on.
- Items with no maker's barcode have a clinic barcode label. Scan that.

**Or type** part of the name and choose from the list:

- the **brand** name (for example "Panadol"),
- the **generic** name (for example "paracetamol"),
- the **strength** (for example "500"),
- or the item code (SKU).

Each result shows:

- the name, with a small badge for prescription-only or controlled medicines,
- the strength, form and generic name,
- the price, for example "LKR 540.00 / Box of 30",
- the stock here, for example "2 × Box of 30 + 5 Tablet in stock", or **Out of stock**,
- the nearest expiry, for example "Exp 03/2027". Amber means it expires within about 3 months. Red means it has expired.

A result that is greyed out cannot be sold at this counter. It says **Not sold here** or **No price**. Tell the Admin if a common item shows this.

If nothing is found the till says **No items found.** Try a shorter word, or the generic name.

## 6. Quantities, units and packs

Each line on the bill has:

- **minus** and **plus** buttons, and a box to type the quantity,
- a **unit** list when the item can be sold in more than one unit (for example **Tablet** or **Box of 30**),
- a **bin** button to remove the line.

Things to know:

- **Whole numbers only.** You can sell 3 tablets or 1 box, never half a tablet or half a box. If you type a part number the till says it is sold in whole units only.
- **A box and loose tablets** of the same medicine can be on the same bill as two lines.
- **Syrups** are sold by the bottle at General Pharmacy. Loose ml is only sold at the OPD counter.
- If a line asks for more than is in stock, the line shows **Only 3 in stock.** Do not sell what is not on the shelf. Check the shelf and tell the Admin.

To start the bill again, press the **bin** button next to **Pay** (**Clear cart**).

## 7. Batch and expiry on each line

Under every medicine line the till shows which batch it will sell, for example **Batch PN2611 exp 03/2027**.

- The system picks the batch itself, **earliest expiry first**. You do not need to choose.
- Take the pack from the shelf with that batch number if you can.
- If a line says something like **5 Tablet not in a batch that can be sold**, there is not enough stock in date. Do not sell expired stock. Tell the Admin.
- Expired batches are never sold.

The printed receipt shows the batch number but not the expiry date. This is the clinic's choice.

## 8. Prescription details

When the bill has a **prescription-only** or **controlled** medicine, a **Prescription** button (F7) appears above the bill.

1. Press **Prescription** (or F7).
2. **Prescriber**: the doctor's name. It may already be filled in with the clinic's usual doctor. Change it if the prescription is from someone else.
3. **Rx reference (optional)**: the prescription number, if there is one.
4. Press **Done**.

The details apply to every prescription line on the bill. If one line has a different prescriber, press **No prescriber entered** (or the prescriber shown) under that line and type it there. **Same as sale** puts it back.

**The prescriber is required.** If you press **Pay** before entering it, the **Prescription** box opens instead, in red. Fill in the prescriber and press **Continue to payment**.

## 9. Discounts

The clinic does not give discounts, so the till shows you no **Discount** button and no line discount. If a discount is really needed, ask the Admin.

## 10. Take payment

1. Check the **Total**.
2. Press **Pay** (or F9). The **Payment** box opens. It shows **Total due**.
3. Choose how the patient pays:

| Payment       | What to do                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cash          | A cash line is already there. Type the cash handed over, or press **Exact**, or a note button (100, 500, 1,000, 2,000, 5,000).               |
| Card          | Press **Add card**. The amount still to pay fills in. Type the card slip number in **Reference** if you have one. Leave the cash line empty. |
| Bank transfer | Press **Add bank transfer**. The amount fills in. Type the transfer reference in **Reference (required)**. Leave the cash line empty.        |
| Split         | Type the cash part in the cash line first, then press **Add card** or **Add bank transfer**. The new line fills in what is still to pay.     |

4. Check the box at the bottom:
    - **Still to pay** (red): not enough has been entered yet.
    - **Change due** (green): give this change back from the drawer.
5. Press **Complete sale** (or Ctrl + Enter).

Rules the till checks:

- **Change is only given from cash.** A card or bank transfer cannot be more than the amount due.
- **A bank transfer needs its reference.** Without it the till says **Enter the bank transfer reference.** and will not complete the sale.
- There is no credit. Every bill is paid in full before it is completed.

## 11. Receipt

After **Complete sale** you see **Sale complete** with the invoice number (for example **Invoice T1-000123**), the **Total** and the **Change given**.

1. Press **Print receipt**. The 80 mm receipt prints straight away on the receipt printer.
2. Hand it to the patient with the medicines.
3. Press **New sale**. The search box is ready for the next patient.

If a small receipt window stays open after printing, close it.

## 12. Recent sales and reprints

1. Press **Recent sales** (or F6). It lists the sales on T1 from your shift and today.
2. Type an invoice number in the search box to find one sale.
3. Press **Reprint** next to the sale.

The reprint is marked **\*\*\* REPRINT \*\*\*** and the system logs who reprinted it. Recent sales does not work while the till is offline.

## 13. Returns, refunds and cancelled bills

Cashiers cannot do returns, refunds or cancel (void) a bill. **Ask the Admin.** The Admin does them in the admin panel, and the refund slip prints from there.

If you made a mistake on a bill **before** pressing **Complete sale**, just fix the line or clear the bill. After it is completed, call the Admin.

## 14. When the internet goes down

The top bar shows the connection:

- **Online** (green): everything is normal.
- **Offline** (amber): the till cannot reach the server. You can keep selling.
- **Offline – 2 sales waiting to sync**: the till is holding 2 sales to send later.

**While offline:**

1. Keep selling as normal: search, scan, prescription, payment and receipt all work.
2. After **Complete sale** the till says **Saved on this till. It will sync when the connection is back.** The receipt still prints.
3. When the internet comes back, the till sends the sales by itself. You can also press **Sync now**.

**What does not work offline:**

- **Recent sales** and reprints.
- **Close shift**. You can only close the shift once every offline sale has been sent. The button is greyed out; holding the mouse over it shows **Sync this till's offline sales before closing the shift**.
- **Stock requests**.

**Do not, while sales are waiting to sync:**

- sign out, press **Change till**, or clear Chrome's history or data. The sales are stored in this browser on this PC. If you try to sign out, the till warns you that sales have not reached the server yet. Press **Cancel**.
- If you are signed out while the internet is down, you cannot sign in again until it is back.

**Messages you might see:**

| Message                                                                    | What to do                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Offline sales are not set up on this till. Ask the owner to set it up.** | Tell the Admin. Until it is done, this till cannot sell offline.                         |
| **This till has no offline invoice numbers left. Reconnect to get more.**  | The till needs the internet before it can sell more offline. Use the other till or wait. |
| **2 sales refused by the server – press Sync now to retry**                | Press **Sync now**. If it stays, tell the Admin.                                         |

## 15. Close your shift and count the cash

At the end of your shift, while the till is **Online** and no sales are waiting:

1. Press **Close shift**.
2. Count all the cash in the drawer, notes and coins.
3. Type the total in **Counted cash (LKR)**.
4. The box shows **Expected cash** and the **Difference**. Green 0.00 means the drawer is right. Red means it is over or short. Count again if it is red.
5. Press **Close shift**.
6. The **Shift closed** summary shows your bills, sales, refunds, **Net sales**, voided bills, each payment method, **Float + cash − refunds**, **Expected cash**, **Counted cash** and the **Difference**.
7. Press **Print the shift summary**. Hand the drawer in with the printed summary.
8. Press **Done**, then **Sign out**.

Card and bank transfer payments are not in the drawer. They are listed on the summary for checking against the card machine and bank.

## 16. Stock requests

Stock requests are made at the OPD counter, to ask General Pharmacy for stock; T1 has no **Stock requests** button. When one arrives, the Admin sends the stock from the admin panel.

## 17. Common problems

| Problem                                                   | What to do                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| The scanner does nothing                                  | Press F2 (or click the search box) and scan again. Check the scanner cable.                                                    |
| An item scans but shows **Not sold here** or **No price** | Do not sell it. Tell the Admin.                                                                                                |
| **Only 3 in stock.** but the shelf has more               | Sell what the system shows. Tell the Admin to check the stock.                                                                 |
| **Pay** is greyed out                                     | Wait a moment for the total to update. Read any red message above the button.                                                  |
| Pay opens the **Prescription** box                        | Enter the prescriber, then **Continue to payment**.                                                                            |
| **Enter the bank transfer reference.**                    | Type the reference in the bank transfer line.                                                                                  |
| The receipt does not print                                | Check the printer has paper and is switched on. Reprint from **Recent sales**. If a print box appears on screen, call support. |
| The till says **Offline**                                 | Keep selling. See section 14.                                                                                                  |
| **Close shift** is greyed out                             | The till is offline or still has sales to sync. Wait until it is **Online**, press **Sync now**.                               |
| A patient wants to return something                       | Ask the Admin.                                                                                                                 |
| You sold the wrong thing and the sale is complete         | Do not ring it up again. Ask the Admin.                                                                                        |
| You forgot your password                                  | Ask the Admin to set a new one for you.                                                                                        |
